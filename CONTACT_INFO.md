## 🎯 **Best Practices for Contacting Us**

### **Before You Email**
1. **Check FAQ** - [FAQ.md](./FAQ.md) may have your answer
2. **Review documentation** - Guides cover most common topics
3. **Search website** - Information may already be available
4. **Try troubleshooting** - Basic steps may resolve your issue

### **Writing Effective Emails**
**Subject Line Format**:
- **Urgent issues**: 🆘 URGENT - [Brief Description]
- **Technical support**: Technical Support - [Issue Type]
- **Content updates**: Content - [Page/Topic]
- **General questions**: General Question - [Topic]

**Email Body**:
- **Be specific** about what you need
- **Include relevant details** (dates, names, error messages)
- **State urgency level** if time-sensitive
- **Provide contact information** for follow-up

**What to Avoid**:
- **Vague subject lines** like "Question" or "Help"
- **Multiple unrelated topics** in one email
- **Missing key information** we need to help you
- **Urgent requests** that aren't actually urgent

## 🔄 **Follow-Up Protocol**

### **If You Don't Hear Back**
**Normal Priority**:
- **Wait 72 hours** before following up
- **Reply to original email** with "Following up"
- **Include original message** for context

**High Priority**:
- **Wait 24 hours** before following up
- **Mark as urgent** if still time-sensitive
- **Consider phone** for critical issues

**Critical Issues**:
- **Follow up after 4 hours** if no response
- **Contact alternative methods** if available
- **Escalate to board members** if needed

### **Status Updates**
- **We'll update you** on complex issues within 24 hours
- **You'll receive confirmation** when your request is received
- **We'll notify you** when your issue is resolved
- **Follow-up emails** will reference your original request
