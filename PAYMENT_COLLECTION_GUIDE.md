# Katy Pride - Payment Collection Guide

## Overview
All payments are collected through **Stripe Checkout** and deposited directly to your bank account.

---

## Payment Flow

### For Vendors (Celebration 2026)
1. Vendor fills form at `/vendor-signup`
2. CRM creates contact in GrowthSphere360
3. Stripe Checkout session created
4. Vendor pays via credit card
5. **Funds deposited to your Stripe account → Bank account**

### For 5K Sponsors
1. Sponsor fills form at `/sponsor-5k`
2. CRM creates contact
3. Two options:
   - **Immediate Payment:** Stripe Checkout (funds to your account)
   - **Invoice Request:** Manual invoice sent later
4. Payment confirmation updates CRM

### For Donations
- Donate button links to `/donate` page
- Form submits to CRM → Stripe Checkout
- Funds go directly to your account

---

## Accessing Your Money

### Stripe Dashboard
- **URL:** https://dashboard.stripe.com
- **Login:** Use your Stripe account credentials
- **Payouts:** View when funds transfer to your bank

### Bank Account
- Funds arrive in 2-7 business days
- Check Stripe dashboard for payout schedule
- All major cards accepted (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)

---

## Fees

**Stripe Processing Fees:**
- 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (standard)
- Example: $275 vendor fee = $7.98 Stripe fee, you receive $267.02

**No Additional Platform Fees:**
- 100% of funds go to Katy Pride
- No fees from GrowthSphere360
- No fees from the website

---

## Reporting

### In Stripe Dashboard:
- **Payments:** View all transactions
- **Customers:** See who paid
- **Payouts:** Track transfers to bank
- **Reports:** Export data for accounting

### In GrowthSphere360 (CRM):
- **Contacts:** Filter by `vendor`, `sponsor`, `donor`
- **Tags:** See who paid vs. pending
- **Custom Fields:** View payment status, amounts

---

## Security

- **PCI Compliant:** Stripe handles all card data
- **SSL Encrypted:** All transactions secure
- **No Card Storage:** We never store card numbers
- **Fraud Protection:** Stripe's built-in fraud detection

---

## Refunds

Process refunds in Stripe Dashboard:
1. Find the payment
2. Click "Refund"
3. Funds returned to customer's card (5-10 business days)
