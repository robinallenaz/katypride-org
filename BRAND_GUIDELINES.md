# Katy Pride Brand Guidelines

## Overview
This document defines the brand identity and visual guidelines for Katy Pride to ensure consistency across all materials.

## Logo
- **Primary Logo**: Katy Pride logo with text (Cloudinary: [65ad7fd64707829ac5cdbe0d_epa64u](https://res.cloudinary.com/dpus8jzix/image/upload/v1769621138/65ad7fd64707829ac5cdbe0d_epa64u.png))
- **Logo Variations**: Square/cropped version for tight spaces
- **Minimum Size**: 40px height for digital, 0.5" for print
- **Clear Space**: Minimum 0.5x logo height on all sides
- **Usage Rules**: Do not stretch, distort, or alter colors. Use on clean backgrounds for maximum impact.

## Social Media Guidelines

### Content Guidelines
- **Allowed Content**: Graphics, images, reels, and videos
- **Articles**: Only if directly related to Katy Pride or affecting LGBTQ population
- **Political Content**: Partisan political statements, comments, shares, likes are prohibited
- **Source Requirements**: Only from actively paying sponsors, affiliated partners, or closely followed topics
- **Accuracy**: All content must be truthful and fact-checked
- **Citations**: Sources must be cited when information is not directly from Katy Pride
- **Prohibited Content**: Sensitive, graphic, profane language, or private information
- **Religious Holidays**: Will post intentionally about diverse religious holidays aligning with organization standards
- **Inclusive Holidays**: Will acknowledge inclusive federal and social holidays aligning with mission and vision
- **Board Process**: Board members must complete Social Post Request form for content consideration
- **Dispute Resolution**: Final discretion rests with Communications Chair, Secretary, and President

### Engagement Guidelines
- **Tone**: All engagement must be positive and respectful, adhering to Katy Pride Tone and Voice guidelines
- **Response Time**: Responses to comments and private messages within 24-48 hours by social media admin
- **Partner Engagement**: Can comment on affiliated partner and paying sponsor posts when appropriate
- **Complaint Handling**: Handle professionally, involve Communications Chair and Board President for guidance
- **Safety Concerns**: Document and report to President, who will contact authorities if necessary
- **Comment Visibility**: All comments remain visible unless safety concern and President advises otherwise
- **Negative Comments**: Admins and board members should refrain from replying to negative comments

## Voice & Tone
### Tone
- **Festive**: Celebratory, energetic, joyful
- **Inclusive**: Welcoming to all identities and backgrounds
- **Hopeful**: Forward-looking, inspiring, positive
- **Fearless**: Courageous, unapologetic, strong
- **Optimistic**: Bright, confident, encouraging

### Voice
- **Bold**: Confident, clear, impactful messaging
- **Authentic**: Genuine, real, true to ourselves
- **Transparent**: Open, honest, straightforward
- **Welcoming**: Friendly, inviting, approachable
- **Equitable**: Fair, just, balanced representation

## Imagery
- **Style**: Authentic, candid community photography with vibrant colors
- **Content**: Diverse community members, events, celebrations, advocacy moments
- **Treatment**: Minimal filters, maintain true colors, rainbow overlays for celebration content

## Layout & Composition
- **Grid System**: 12-column grid, 8px baseline rhythm
- **Spacing**: 8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px increments
- **Alignment**: Left-aligned text, center-aligned headers, consistent margins

- **Button Styles**
- **Form Elements**

### Social Media
- **Profile Images**: Katy Pride logo
- **Post Templates**: Brand colors, pillar-specific borders, consistent typography

## Applications
### Correct Usage
- Event flyers with green borders for Events pillar content
- Social media posts using pillar-specific colors as borders
- Business cards with logo on clean white background
- Celebration materials using rainbow gradient as background overlay
- Advocacy communications with red accent colors

### Incorrect Usage
- Stretching or distorting the logo
- Using logo on busy or patterned backgrounds
- Mixing pillar colors for non-pillar content
- Using rainbow gradient for pillar-specific communications
- Altering brand colors or using unapproved colors
- Placing logo too close to other elements (insufficient clear space)

## File Formats
- **Logo Files**: SVG (vector), PNG (transparent), JPG (white background)
- **Color Profiles**: RGB for digital, CMYK for print
- **Naming Conventions**: katypride-element-version.ext (e.g., katypride-logo-primary.svg)
