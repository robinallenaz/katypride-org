## 🚨 **Critical Issues (Fix Now)**

### **Website Not Loading**
**Problem**: Website shows error page or won't load
**Solutions**:
1. **Check your internet connection** - Try other websites
2. **Clear browser cache** - Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
3. **Try incognito/private browser** - Rules out extension issues
4. **Check status page** - [Vercel Status](https://www.vercel-status.com/)
5. **Report if still down** - Contact technical support

**⏱️ Time to fix**: Usually 1-2 minutes

---

### **Forms Not Submitting**
**Problem**: Submit button doesn't work or shows error
**Quick Checks**:
- ✅ All required fields filled (marked with *)
- ✅ Valid email format (name@domain.com)
- ✅ Valid phone format (555-123-4567)
- ✅ Not a bot (leave hidden fields empty)

**Solutions**:
1. **Refresh page** and try again
2. **Check error messages** below form fields
3. **Try different browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
4. **Wait 1 minute** if submitting rapidly (rate limiting)

**⏱️ Time to fix**: Usually 2-3 minutes

## 📧 **Email & Communication Issues**

### **Not Receiving Confirmation Emails**
**Common Causes**:
- **Check spam/junk folder** - Most common issue
- **Wrong email address** - Typos happen
- **Email delays** - Can take 5-10 minutes
- **Blocked by domain** - School/work email filters

**Solutions**:
1. **Add info@katypride.org to contacts**
2. **Check all email folders** (Promotions, Social, Spam)
3. **Whitelist our domain** with your IT department
4. **Try different email address** if needed

**⏱️ Time to fix**: 5-10 minutes

### **Want to Unsubscribe from Newsletter**
**Options**:
- **Click "Unsubscribe"** link in any newsletter email
- **Email us directly**: info@katypride.org
- **Fill form**: [Contact Form](/contact) with "Unsubscribe" request

**⏱️ Time to fix**: 1-2 minutes

## 💳 **Payment & Donation Issues**

### **Payment Declined**
**Common Reasons**:
- **Insufficient funds**
- **Wrong card info** (number, CVV, expiry)
- **Bank blocked transaction**
- **International card** (some banks block)

**Solutions**:
1. **Check card details** carefully
2. **Try different card**
3. **Call your bank** to unblock
4. **Use PayPal** if available

**⏱️ Time to fix**: 3-5 minutes

### **Donation Receipt Needed**
**Options**:
- **Check email** - Receipt sent automatically
- **Search inbox** for "katypride" or "donation"
- **Request copy**: info@katypride.org
- **Include**: Name, amount, date, email used

**⏱️ Time to get receipt**: 1-2 minutes

## 🔧 **Technical Issues**

### **Can't Log In to Admin Panel**
**Solutions**:
1. **Check URL**: `https://katypride-strapi.onrender.com/admin`
2. **Reset password** - Click "Forgot password"
3. **Clear browser cache**
4. **Try different browser**
5. **Check credentials** - Case-sensitive

**⏱️ Time to fix**: 2-5 minutes

### **Images Not Uploading**
**Common Issues**:
- **File too large** - Keep under 5MB
- **Wrong format** - Use JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Slow internet** - Try better connection
- **Browser issues** - Try Chrome or Firefox

**Best Practices**:
- **Size**: 1200x630px for event images
- **Format**: JPG for photos, PNG for logos
- **Quality**: High but compressed
- **Name**: Descriptive (e.g., "pride-celebration-2026.jpg")

**⏱️ Time to fix**: 3-5 minutes
